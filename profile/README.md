<h1 align="center">RiskThing</h1>

<p align="center"><b>Risk intelligence for decisions you can't take back.</b></p>

For founders and engineering leads building things that have to work the first time. RiskThing captures risks, surfaces options, records decisions, and tells the right people. Turbopumps to term sheets: your whole register on one NASA-style 5×5, live, not rebuilt in PowerPoint.

## Bring your AI agent

[**riskthing-skill**](https://github.com/riskthing/riskthing-skill) teaches an AI agent to write, score and track risks and mitigations in RiskThing, NASA RMH or DoD RIO style. Connect it to RiskThing and the agent keeps your register current: it captures risks as they come up during other work, builds a first register from an interview or a plan, and runs check-ins.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/riskthing/riskthing-skill ~/.claude/skills/riskthing
```

- **Closed by default.** Your admin decides whether agents connect at all, and who.
- **Your authority, never more.** Agents use tokens bound to a person, see only what that person sees, and stop on revocation.
- **Every action attributed.** Agent changes wait in your Agent activity list for review.

## Why teams use it

- **Private by default.** Every risk starts as a draft, invisible even to your admins until you share it, so the half-formed worry gets logged.
- **Decisions, not just lists.** Mitigations link to the risks they cover, and dispositions put each call on the record.
- **Secrets stay secret.** AWS GovCloud from day one. Full data export anytime; no lock-in.
- **Built by a former NASA Flight Director.** The same discipline that flies missions, without the mission-control bureaucracy.
